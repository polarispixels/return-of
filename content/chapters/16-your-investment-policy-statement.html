An investment policy statement is the written contract you make with yourself, so that the rules are chosen in calm and followed under pressure.

What it is and why you write it

An investment policy statement (IPS) is a short document that says what your money is for, how it is divided, what you may do with it and when you must stop. Institutions use one so that decisions do not change with the mood of the week. You can do the same for yourself.

It matters because the worst decisions happen when you are excited or afraid. A written rule is a decision made earlier, by a calmer you. In this method the IPS is written in Level 1 of the Ladder, before any active trade. You own it, you change it only on review, and you do not change it in the middle of a trade.

Promotion and demotion

Write these rules in the IPS exactly as the method states them.

  • Promotion rule: positive expectancy and at least 90% adherence to your own rules, shown in the journal.
  • Demotion rule: 10 trading days without the morning routine sends the active slice back to the core.

Level 4 adds a separate gate: about 50 paper trades with positive expectancy before any real long options or 0DTE trades. You unlock levels with proof, not with a feeling of readiness, and you lose them by neglect.

Review and revise

Set a review date and keep it. At review, read the journal, check each number against what happened, and change the IPS on paper before changing it in practice. Include the rules of the road from Chapter 14: your trade cap, wash sale tracking and settled-funds rule belong in the document. Date every version so you can see how your rules grew.

Back to the beginning

Every section above serves one rule: protect the money before you try to grow it. The buckets keep near-term money safe. The sizing rule caps each loss. The trade cap and the tuition budget limit the damage from a bad week. The demotion rule returns money to the core when your attention lapses. The return of your capital comes first. The return on it follows only if the first is intact.

A written rule is a decision made earlier, by a calmer you.

Open questions
